# About BeanLab Coffee

BeanLab Coffee is a specialty coffee shop founded in 2021 in downtown Portland, Oregon. We focus on single-origin beans, manual brewing methods, and educating customers about coffee origin and flavor profiles.

## Our story
Founded by Marcus Chen and Sofia Reyes, two former chemical engineers who fell in love with coffee science during a trip to Colombia. BeanLab is named after our obsession with experimenting with brewing variables — temperature, grind size, water composition — to extract the best flavor from every bean.

## What makes us different
- All beans are single-origin and traceable to the farm of origin.
- We roast in-house every Monday and Thursday.
- Our baristas pass a 6-week internal training program before serving customers.
- We offer free brewing classes every Saturday morning.
