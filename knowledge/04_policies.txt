# BeanLab Coffee – Policies

## Refund and return policy

### In-store purchases
- Drinks: if you're unsatisfied for any reason, we'll remake your drink at no cost within the same visit.
- Food items: full refund or replacement available within 30 minutes of purchase.
- Whole bean coffee: opened bags cannot be refunded in-store. Unopened bags can be returned within 7 days with a receipt for a full refund.

### Online and shipped orders
- Unopened items can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Opened bags of coffee cannot be returned, but contact us at feedback@beanlabcoffee.com — we may offer store credit on a case-by-case basis.
- Damaged shipments must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. We'll send a replacement at no extra cost.
- Subscription orders can be cancelled, paused, or modified at any time from your online account.

## Shipping policy

- We ship anywhere in the continental United States.
- Standard shipping (3-5 business days): flat $7.
- Free standard shipping on orders over $50.
- Expedited shipping (1-2 business days): $15.
- We do not ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or international destinations at this time.
- Orders placed before 11:00 AM Pacific ship the same business day.
- We do not ship on weekends or US federal holidays.

## Gift card policy

- Gift cards available in denominations of $10, $25, $50, and $100.
- Physical and digital gift cards available.
- Gift cards never expire.
- Cannot be redeemed for cash except where required by law.
- Lost physical gift cards cannot be replaced.

## Privacy policy summary

- We collect minimal personal information: name, email, and payment info for online orders.
- We never sell customer data to third parties.
- Marketing emails are opt-in only and can be unsubscribed from at any time.
- For our full privacy policy, see www.beanlabcoffee.com/privacy.

## Accessibility

- Both BeanLab locations are wheelchair accessible.
- Accessible restrooms available at both locations.
- Braille menus available upon request.
- Service animals always welcome.

## Code of conduct

We're committed to a safe and welcoming environment for everyone. BeanLab does not tolerate:
- Harassment of any kind (staff or customers)
- Discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, or any other identity
- Disruptive or threatening behavior

Customers violating this code may be asked to leave. Repeated violations result in permanent ban.

## Lost and found

Lost items are kept for 30 days at the counter of the location where they were found. After 30 days, unclaimed items are donated to local charities. To inquire about a lost item, email hello@beanlabcoffee.com with date, location, and description.

## Reservations

We do not take table reservations at the main shop (first-come, first-served seating). For private events at the roastery location, see our events FAQ or email events@beanlabcoffee.com.
