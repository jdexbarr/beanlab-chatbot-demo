# BeanLab Coffee – Frequently Asked Questions

## General questions

**Q: Do you have WiFi?**
A: Yes, free WiFi is available for all customers. Network: BeanLab-Guest. Password is printed on every receipt.

**Q: Is BeanLab a good place to work remotely?**
A: Absolutely. We have power outlets at every table, free WiFi, and a quiet atmosphere on weekdays. We ask remote workers to purchase at least one item every 2 hours to support the business.

**Q: Do you allow pets?**
A: Service animals are always welcome. Friendly leashed dogs are welcome on our outdoor patio.

**Q: Is there parking nearby?**
A: Street parking is available on NW Glisan Street (metered). Free parking after 7 PM and on Sundays. There's also a public garage one block east on NW 4th Avenue ($3/hour).

**Q: Do you have outdoor seating?**
A: Yes, our main location has a covered patio with 8 tables, open year-round (heaters in winter).

## Ordering and payment

**Q: How do I order ahead?**
A: You can order through our website (www.beanlabcoffee.com/order) or via the BeanLab app (iOS and Android). Most orders are ready in 5-10 minutes.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Cash, all major credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and BeanLab gift cards. We do not accept personal checks.

**Q: Do you offer a loyalty program?**
A: Yes. Buy 9 drinks, get the 10th free. You can track your stamps through our app or with a physical loyalty card. New members get a free drink on their birthday.

**Q: Can I get a discount for bringing my own cup?**
A: Yes. Bring any reusable cup and get $0.50 off any drink.

## Delivery and pickup

**Q: Do you offer delivery?**
A: Yes. We deliver through Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Grubhub within a 3-mile radius. Direct orders for delivery over $30 are eligible for free in-house delivery (call us directly).

**Q: How long does delivery take?**
A: Typical delivery time is 25-40 minutes depending on platform and time of day.

**Q: Can I schedule pickup for a specific time?**
A: Yes, through our app you can schedule pickup up to 24 hours in advance.

## Dietary and allergies

**Q: Do you offer dairy-free options?**
A: Yes. We offer oat, almond, soy, coconut, and lactose-free milk (all +$0.75).

**Q: Are any of your pastries gluten-free?**
A: We carry a gluten-free brownie daily. Other gluten-free options rotate seasonally — call ahead to check availability.

**Q: Do you have vegan options?**
A: Yes. All our plant-based milks are vegan. Our vegan banana bread and most of our cold brew options are 100% vegan. The veggie wrap is vegan when ordered without cheese.

**Q: Can you accommodate severe nut allergies?**
A: Our kitchen handles tree nuts (almond milk, almond croissants). We take precautions but cannot guarantee allergen-free preparation. Customers with severe allergies should consult our staff before ordering.

## Brewing classes and events

**Q: Do you offer brewing classes?**
A: Yes. Every Saturday at 10:00 AM we run a 90-minute brewing class. Cost: $35 per person. Includes hands-on practice with V60, AeroPress, and French Press, plus a 4 oz bag of beans to take home. Booking required via our website.

**Q: Can I rent the space for a private event?**
A: Yes. Our roastery location (218 SE Stark Street) is available for private events on Sundays after 5 PM. Capacity: 30 people. Pricing starts at $400 for a 3-hour rental, which includes coffee service for 20 guests. Email events@beanlabcoffee.com for booking.

## Wholesale and beans

**Q: Do you sell whole beans?**
A: Yes. Our single-origin beans and house blend are available in 12 oz bags at the shop and online. We ship anywhere in the continental US (flat $7 shipping; free over $50).

**Q: Do you supply coffee to restaurants or offices?**
A: Yes. We have a wholesale program for businesses ordering 5+ lbs per week. Email wholesale@beanlabcoffee.com for pricing and details.

**Q: Can I subscribe to receive beans monthly?**
A: Yes. We offer monthly subscriptions starting at $20/month for a 12 oz bag delivered to your door. Cancel anytime through your account.

## Returns and complaints

**Q: What if I'm not happy with my drink?**
A: We'll remake it for free, no questions asked. Just bring it back to the counter within the same visit.

**Q: Can I return whole bean coffee if I don't like it?**
A: For online orders, you can return unopened bags within 14 days for a full refund. Opened bags are non-refundable, but contact us — we may offer a partial credit.

**Q: How do I leave feedback or a complaint?**
A: Email feedback@beanlabcoffee.com. We respond within 24 hours during business days.
