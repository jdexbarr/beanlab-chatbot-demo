# BeanLab Coffee – Events, Classes, and Workshops

## Weekly events

### Saturday Brewing Class
- When: Every Saturday at 10:00 AM.
- Duration: 90 minutes.
- Location: Main shop, 412 NW Glisan Street.
- Cost: $35 per person (booking required).
- Includes: hands-on practice with V60, AeroPress, and French Press, plus a 4 oz bag of beans to take home.
- Max participants: 8 per class.
- Booking: www.beanlabcoffee.com/classes
- What to bring: nothing — all equipment and ingredients provided.

### Cupping Tuesdays
- When: Every Tuesday at 6:00 PM.
- Duration: 60 minutes.
- Location: Roastery, 218 SE Stark Street.
- Cost: free (limited to 10 participants).
- Description: a guided coffee tasting where we present 4-6 different origins. Great for understanding flavor profiles.
- Booking: www.beanlabcoffee.com/cuppings

### Latte Art Workshop
- When: First Sunday of every month, 1:00 PM.
- Duration: 2 hours.
- Location: Main shop.
- Cost: $55 per person.
- Includes: theory of milk steaming, hands-on practice, free drink at the end.
- Max participants: 6 per class.
- Booking: www.beanlabcoffee.com/latte-art

## Monthly events

### Roastery Open House
- When: First Saturday of every month, 1:00-4:00 PM.
- Location: Roastery, 218 SE Stark Street.
- Cost: free.
- Description: open visit to see the roasting process, meet the team, taste fresh roasts.

### Origin Spotlight Night
- When: Last Friday of every month, 7:00-9:00 PM.
- Location: Main shop.
- Cost: $25 per person.
- Description: in-depth tasting of a specific origin, with stories about the farm and farmers. Includes 3 brewed cups and small bites.
- Each month features a different region.

## Private events

### Roastery rental
- Available: Sundays after 5:00 PM.
- Capacity: 30 people.
- Pricing: starts at $400 for 3 hours.
- Includes: coffee service for up to 20 guests.
- Add-ons: catering, custom signage, barista showcase available.
- Booking: events@beanlabcoffee.com

### Corporate coffee workshops
- We offer customized coffee workshops for company teams (8-20 people).
- Duration: 90 minutes to 3 hours.
- Pricing: starts at $50 per person.
- Can be hosted at our roastery or at your office (within 10 miles of Portland).
- Booking: events@beanlabcoffee.com

## Seasonal events
- Holiday Roastery Sale: weekend before Thanksgiving, with all beans 20% off.
- Annual Coffee Festival: every May, free public event with multiple roasters from the Pacific Northwest.
- Espresso Championship: every September, open competition for amateur baristas.

For the most up-to-date events calendar, visit www.beanlabcoffee.com/events.
